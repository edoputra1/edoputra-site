# A Place for Makers

tinyshipsailors — or **tss** for short — is a small crew of dads who once worked under the same roof. We were part of the same team, under the same management, until restructuring happened and the group was disbanded. Titles changed. Org charts shifted. But we stuck together.

What connects us is simple:
- We all come from a design background.
- We balance artistic instinct with logical thinking.
- We care deeply about crafting digital products.

In uncertain, post–“let go” times, tss became more than a chat group. It became a support circle. A creative collective. A place to regroup and rebuild.

---

Are we starting a company? Maybe. Maybe not. We’re not interested in becoming a service agency. This is about building products. About experimenting. About making things we actually want to exist. And yes — we’re toying with vibecoding. Everyone else is doing it. Why shouldn’t we?

Personally, I dipped my toes into it by building a Tamagochi-esque desktop companion. I’m old school. Pixel art is a soft spot to me. And I wanted something small, something personal, something I’d actually use. It felt like a good place to start. Learning Xcode, learning Swift, exploring AI-first workflows. The access is wide open. The tools are here. The world really does feel like an oyster right now.

![tinyPet — Work In Progress](/posts/tinyshipsailors/post-img2.jpg)

Vibecoding, though, isn’t as effortless as some make it sound—this probably deserves its own post. It can be costly if you commit seriously. Sure, you can go the zero-cost route — but you’ll need monk-level patience when daily or weekly token limits hit. Not me. I’m not that zen 😪

---

Still, it’s a start. It keeps me grounded. It keeps me hopeful. I haven’t felt this excited about building in a long time.

One of our sailors said it best:
> “The joy of making product is real.”

And I feel it too.
