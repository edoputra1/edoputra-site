# On My Own Two Feet

It's April. Three months since I got my notice. And somewhere between the interviews, I stopped looking for another seat at someone else's table.

> No more office politics. No more restructuring emails.

I thought about it long and hard. Talked it through with my wife. Looked at the numbers. Looked at the risk.

It wasn't dramatic. No movie-moment epiphany. Just a calm, stubborn clarity that this was the move. Just me, my laptop, and whatever I can build with my own hands.

Scary? Yeah.
Does it feel right? Also yeah.

# What's Next

- I'm building two things I actually care about
- A desktop companion that roasts you from time to time
- A content creator tool for people who are into kinetic typography
