# It Happens

I was enjoying the New Year vacation with my wife and kids when a scheduled meeting invite from my employer popped up. You already know how that story ends. The title spoils it anyway.

Officially, it was called organizational restructuring. Unofficially — I was let go.

Just like that.

2026, new year, not-so-fresh start, another chapter, mood shattered. Vacation energy gone. That strange quiet after impact settling in.

And yeah... it sucked, not gonna lie.

---

# Life Goes On

Now I’m serving my notice period. The atmosphere in the office has shifted, definitely. No one says anything out loud or directly, but you can feel it — that subtle 'dissonant feeling' in the air, conversations slightly shorter, emails slightly feel different.

Still, life doesn’t pause for long. No time for sulking. I started applying everywhere. Interviews here and there. Some awkward. Some promising. Some draining. But enduring a faked one is just another level...

After a while, I hit that point:

> Ah, forget it.
> No more nonsense.
> Time to pivot.

---

# Vibe-coding

I used to host my portfolio on Carbonmade. Simple, basic, functional. New year means new subscription cycle — and I didn’t feel like paying for a static site again.

So I asked myself:

> Why not just build it?

I have a very basic (I mean really basic) understanding of web development. Enough to know what’s going on with HTML, CSS and JS. Not enough to call myself a frontend engineer.

But enough.
That's the keyword.

So I did it.

No Webflow. No Framer. No frameworks. No React. No Tailwind. No opinionated structures I didn’t choose myself.

Just *vanilla* everything.
Well, partly because I’m not fluent in modern frameworks 😅
Mostly because I wanted full control—thanks to my designer's ego.

And yes, ChatGPT assisted me a lot. Call it vibecoding if you want. What you’re seeing now? It works, right?
