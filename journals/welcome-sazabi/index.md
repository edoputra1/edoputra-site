# Big-Red-Chonky-Boi Is (Finally) Done!

Man... this Master Grade MSN-04 Sazabi (Ver. Ka), a 1/100 scale kit originally released in 2013, doesn't feel like a "normal" MG at all. It feels like a step above — more advanced, more complex. Even the box is huge, easily twice the size of what I'm used to (at least compared to my RE/100 Lindwurm or 1/100 Hi-Resolution Model Astray Red Frame).

Gemini said it better, Sazabi is often described as a "Perfect Grade masquerading as a Master Grade," this kit is massive, detailed, and tedious due to the high number of parts (600+) and inner frame work.

> So why chose Sazabi?
>
> I first noticed Sazabi not from the OVA, but from Linkin Park's Somewhere I Belong music video. And man, it looked so dope — so, I wanted one.

---

![Sazabi comparison](/posts/welcome-sazabi/img-1.jpg)

It took me a while to finish this one — not just because it's complex, but also because I couldn't focus on it in one sitting. Between office work and freelance stuff, it kept getting pushed aside. But now that I've finally had some free time (all the free time, lol), I managed to complete it.

It's also the most expensive out of the three. And yeah, it shows. The detailing — especially on the legs and torso — is insane. It took me quite a bit of time just to get through that part, not to mention adding decals and finishing touches. Even the fingers are fully articulated, so you can pose them into a proper fist if you want.

---

![Sazabi build](/posts/welcome-sazabi/img-2.jpg)

It's also the tallest of the bunch, which isn't surprising considering the box size. It's got some weight to it too — it stands really well on its own without any issues, unlike the Lindwurm.

I did a bit of painting on the weapons — added some yellow and red to break up the gray — and it turned out pretty decent. Definitely makes me want to invest in an airbrush for a cleaner finish next time.

![Sazabi legs](/posts/welcome-sazabi/img-3.jpg)

Char's mobile suit looks just as badass on my desk as it does in Char's Counterattack. Big, bold, and intimidating — exactly how it should be.

Welcome, Sazabi.
